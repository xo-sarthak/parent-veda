# PVSectionEyebrow

The small upper-case label above a section — ParentVeda's signature, and the
app's only routine use of the brand violet.

```jsx
<PVSectionEyebrow>Where to go</PVSectionEyebrow>
<PVSectionEyebrow on="field">This week</PVSectionEyebrow>
```

Name the section after what the reader gets, never after a content type:
"WHERE TO GO" beats "LINKS".
