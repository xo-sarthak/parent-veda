# PVFactBlock

One fact, or an honest statement that a fact is coming.

```jsx
<PVFactBlock label="Typical cost" value="₹1,800 – ₹3,500" />
<PVFactBlock label="Costs near you" placeholder="Once we have them verified for your city." />
```

The placeholder form states the value and is **not tappable** — a placeholder
that looks tappable and does nothing teaches the reader that taps do nothing.
