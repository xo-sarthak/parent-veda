# PVCard

A white surface on the page ground, with a shadow tinted to the ground rather
than black.

```jsx
<PVCard onClick={() => {}}>
  <PVText role="cardTitle">Anomaly scan</PVText>
  <PVText role="bodySm">Usually between weeks 18 and 22.</PVText>
</PVCard>
```

**No card inside a card.** Reach for PVFactBlock instead.
