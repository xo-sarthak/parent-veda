# Partner logos (demo)

Drop PNGs here with EXACTLY these names and they appear across every brand
surface automatically. Until a file exists, `BrandMark` falls back to a
monogram tile in the brand's colour, so nothing looks broken either way.

Guidance: square-ish, transparent background, roughly 256x256 or larger.
They are rendered small (20-44px) and rounded.

THESE ARE DEMO PLACEHOLDERS. None of these companies is a ParentVeda partner.
Replace with licensed assets from contracted partners before anything
public-facing, and remove any logo you do not have the right to use.
