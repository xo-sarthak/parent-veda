## Safety observations (our reading, not medical advice)
- Prenatal post: Frog pose (fists pressed into the navel, folding forward) is listed for the first trimester.
  Seated forward bend and Downward Dog are listed for the second. Nothing says to avoid lying flat on the back
  later in pregnancy, and there are no stop-signs (bleeding, fluid, contractions, dizziness, reduced movement).
  No contraindication list (placenta praevia, pre-eclampsia, cervical weakness). Only "consult your doctor before straining".
- Postpartum post: suggests Boat pose and Bridge for core work with diastasis recti, and "Forearm Plank" as the gentle
  option. Framing is weight loss.
- Timing contradiction: postpartum post says gentle yoga at 4-6 weeks after a vaginal birth and 8-12 weeks after a C-section;
  the C-section post says to wait about 4 months.
- Claims that break our clinical rules: "Reverse PCOD/PCOS", "Improves AMH Levels", "10,000+ people have Conceived
  Naturally", male page "Reverses erectile dysfunction", "Improves sperm count", FAQ "conditions are a result of a
  prolonged bad lifestyle". Store tagline "Cure pcos/pcod". Store body has a proper wellness disclaimer.
- Instructor credentials shown: PG diplomas in yoga, one YCB Level 3 (Ministry of AYUSH). No obstetric or physio reviewer named.
