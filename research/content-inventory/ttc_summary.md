# TTC — what the numbers say

**Counted, not estimated.** 7 doors, 170 tiles, 52 long reads (~79,000 words),
16 films (142 minutes, 75 chapters written, none shot), 64 carousel cards,
and about 250 short data entries (products, tests, trackers, practices,
insights, missions, milestones).

## The ten biggest asks

1. **16 films.** 142 minutes. Scripts and chapters exist; no footage does.
2. **52 reads rewritten or reviewed.** 79,000 words carrying doctor bylines
   nobody earned. This is the stage's whole claim to being trustworthy.
3. **A named clinician** to sign 52 reads, 6 tool outcome sets, 10 test
   explainers and 6 vaccine entries.
4. **7 door hero photographs.** All Unsplash today.
5. **170 rail-card illustrations** — the flat tinted blocks are the reason the
   doors look unfinished.
6. **12 practice recordings** from scripts that already exist.
7. **14 real offerings** with real experts, syllabi and prices.
8. **10 product packshots and real SKUs.**
9. **Current INR lab pricing** for the 10-test library, refreshed periodically.
10. **64 carousel card illustrations** on the three doors that use them.

## Old versus V3

* The **doors** (`lib/ttc/focus/`) are the V3 shape and are what the V3 home
  opens. Everything marked `v3` is live in the current flow.
* The **hubs** (`ttc_hubs.dart`, 7 hubs, 14 needs, 7 hero film slots) are the
  older shape. Some are still reachable. **Do not commission the 7 hub hero
  films until someone confirms the hubs still ship** — that is the one real
  ambiguity in this stage.
* `ttc_home_version.dart` switches between the old home and V3, so both exist
  in the binary today.
