# Shared, commerce and platform — what the numbers say

Smaller in volume, larger in consequence. Most of this is not writing but
**agreements and verification**: sponsors, experts, legal text, and Meta
template approval.

## The biggest asks

1. **12 real experts** with credentials, registrations, photographs and
   consent. Every consult in every stage points at an invented person today.
2. **Lawyer-drafted privacy, terms and medical disclaimer** for a health app
   in India.
3. **Signed sponsor agreements.** The 4 partner logos (Apollo, Cetaphil,
   Fisher-Price, Himalaya) are demo brands, not customers, and the launch
   carousel shows 4 placeholder slides.
4. **WhatsApp templates submitted to Meta.** The words gate the feature; the
   engine is already built and applied as migrations 0015 to 0018.
5. **40 bracket intent marks**, drawn in code today.
6. **A hub decision.** 24 hubs across the stages each declare a hero film
   slot. The hub shape is the older one. Do not commission 24 films before
   someone says the hubs still ship.
7. **38 room icons** to replace the decorative emoji the community rooms use,
   which break the app's own no-emoji rule.
8. **Community guidelines**, and moderators to enforce them.
9. **Font licence confirmation** for commercial distribution.

## Community is not a content ask, and an earlier draft of this sheet had it
## wrong

A community is written by the people in it. **We do not commission posts.**
What is ours is only the structure and the governance:

* **38 room names and descriptions** — already written, in both languages.
  This list grows on a schedule rather than finishing: a new due-date cohort
  room every month.
* **Community guidelines and a moderation rota** — not in the code today, and
  needed before the first real post.
* **The "question of the week" pulse prompt**, if it ships — a standing
  editorial commitment of roughly 52 prompts a year, not a one-off.

The **50 seed posts** in `community_data.dart` are prototype demo data with
invented authors and invented like counts. They are a thing to **delete before
launch** (or hide behind a demo flag), not a thing to write more of. The file
labels itself "seed data (prototype)".

## Real assets that already exist

The three ParentVeda marks and the four partner logos in `assets/brand/` are
real files. Everything else in this sheet is placeholder or absent.

## Ambiguity

Whether the **hub** surfaces ship alongside the newer bracket-and-door flow is
the one question that materially changes this sheet, and the code does not
answer it. It is marked `old` throughout rather than guessed.
