# Father mode — what the numbers say

**The father side is thin, and the shape of the thinness is the finding.**
37 weekly files hold 4,839 words between them — about 130 words a week. His
daily content file has exactly **one** day in it, where the mother side has
259. The 60 tales are the only substantial body of father-side writing.

**Some of his content is derived, not authored.** `father_day_derive.dart` and
`father_week_derive.dart` build his scans view, his What-is-next and his
Samvad mirror from her content at build time. Those need no commissioning and
are marked `derived-from-mother` so nobody pays twice for them.

## The biggest asks

1. **A decision before any writing: is his home daily or weekly?** If daily,
   258 day entries are missing. If weekly, the 37 weekly files need to be
   four times their current length.
2. **60 tale covers.** The tales exist; the art does not.
3. **37 weekly heroes.** Her week images cannot be reused — the Slate look is
   deliberately his own.
4. **Audio for the 60 tales**, if his side gets narration.
5. **A film decision** — he has a weekly videos carousel and no catalogue.

## Ambiguity

I could not tell from the code whether the father daily home is meant to carry
one entry per day or one per week. Everything else in this sheet depends on
that answer, so it is flagged rather than guessed.
