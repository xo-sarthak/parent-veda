# Pregnancy — what the numbers say

**This stage is the most finished part of the app, and the numbers say why.**
The weekly spine (37 weeks x 11 fields, 33,760 words) is real and has a full
Hindi twin. The daily content is 311,047 words across 259 days. The per-week
baby illustrations are real assets. Roughly 480,000 words of pregnancy
writing already exist.

## The ten biggest asks

1. **4,886 missing Hindi narration recordings.** 5,684 passages are listed in
   the manifest; 798 are recorded. That is 14% done.
2. **A clinical sign-off on 335 safety entries** — 193 "Can I…?", 27
   conditions, 27 report findings, 19 tests and scans, 17 symptoms, 52 mind
   and mood pieces including a screener.
3. **33,616 words of religious content** reviewed by someone knowledgeable per
   tradition.
4. **37 food-comparison images** to match the finished baby set.
5. **24 product photographs and real SKUs** (24 of the app's 44 Unsplash URLs
   are here).
6. **10 real raga recordings.** One exists.
7. **39 paid offerings** with real instructors, schedules and prices.
8. **Recipe and food photography** — 16 recipes, 63 food entries.
9. **34 belly-and-skin images.** Skin advice without pictures does not work.
10. **English narration**, if wanted, at another 5,684 passages.

## Where old and new could not be told apart

* **Garbh Sanskar** has two data files (`garbh_data.dart`,
  `garbh_rebuild_data.dart`). I could not tell which is live. Resolve before
  commissioning audio or imagery for it.
* **Week 5 "Full"** exists for one week only, behind a toggle. Either it rolls
  out to 36 more weeks or it retires. That decision changes the largest single
  number in this sheet.
* **Journal V1 and V2** both exist; V2 is the keepsake product.
* **Home generations**: classic, Warm Nest (`home_screen_b`) and V3
  (`home_v3_screen`) all exist in the binary.
* **assets/blocks** (6 real PNGs) belongs to the V2 Focus experiment.
* Pregnancy defines **no film catalogue at all**, where TTC defines 16. That is
  a gap in the product, not in the data.
