Severity: **B** = blocker (broken or misleading) · **C** = confusing, inconsistent or wasteful · **P** = polish.

## State of launch readiness

Nothing crashed, no screen failed to load, and every tap walked led somewhere, so the stage **works**. It is **not
yet launch-ready on honesty and on a few answers**, and it is uneven in finish. Four blockers: the home Period button
opens a stock date picker pre-set to today, so one reflex tap restarts her cycle (H1); a made-up doctor ("Dr. Meera
Iyer") with a verified tick endorses a product (H13); every TTC read shows "REVIEWED BY" and a tick for experts who
have not yet been sent their pieces (H14); and several Can I...? answers put a bold "Yes, this is fine" under a
question that asks whether to avoid something (T7). Beyond those, the recurring C-level patterns are: asking for
facts the app already holds (age, cycle length, weight, his semen test — D9, D18, T2), one thing with two or three
names (Sanskar/ritual, fertile days/window, Your cycle/Cycle companion — H15, M3, D16, MB6), unmade films shown as
playable (D3, L3), text-only door tiles and story slides that are mostly empty (D1, D4), leftover purple in headers
and links (MB13, T6), and two product photos that show the wrong product or brand (PR1, PR2). Build 16 (installed
mid-walk) already fixed the Mind & body card layout, the Symptoms and mood top box and search, the switches, and
put photographs on the Fertile window door. Counts, open on build 16: **4 B, 49 C, 30 P** (5 rows marked fixed in 16,
several marked partly fixed).

**Not walked (the phone was needed back):** You tab and its rows, Ask Veda's screen, the Fertile window and Records
tools as tools (both seen on the way), the third read to its end, IVF › Track, After a loss › Understand and Trying
again, Body and cycle › Intimate health and Other conditions, Products › one product page from the Products tab (one
was opened from Today). Findings logged before build 16 that were not re-checked are left as they were.
