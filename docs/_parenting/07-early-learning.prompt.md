# DOOR 1: Something to do today (activity engine) — reuse pp_activities, FILL it

The daily "what to do with my child now" surface. Reads the child's age and serves the age-right activity bank below. This is the retention driver. Each activity is a page: what happens / materials / what it builds / age. Video only where marked.

## Band 0-12 months (play = brain-building, not "learning")
1. **Tummy-time reach** — lay baby on tummy, place a high-contrast toy just out of reach; baby lifts head and reaches. Materials: play mat, one toy. Builds: neck/core strength, visual tracking. Age 0-6m. Video: yes (positioning demo).
2. **Black-and-white card gaze** — hold high-contrast cards ~25cm from baby's face, move slowly side to side. Materials: B&W cards. Builds: visual focus, tracking. Age 0-3m.
3. **Peekaboo** — cover your face, reappear with "peekaboo." Materials: none/a cloth. Builds: object permanence, social smiling. Age 4-12m.
4. **Name-the-object narration** — carry baby around the room naming what you touch ("this is the fan, this is thanda water"). Materials: none. Builds: language exposure. Age 0-12m.
5. **Cause-and-effect rattle** — give a rattle/steel katori and spoon; baby shakes and hears sound. Materials: household. Builds: cause-effect, grasp. Age 4-9m.
6. **Mirror play** — sit baby before a mirror, point and name "baby!". Materials: mirror. Builds: self-recognition beginnings, social. Age 5-12m.
7. **Textured touch basket** — a basket of safe textures (soft cloth, wooden spoon, silicone). Materials: household. Builds: sensory, fine motor. Age 6-12m.
8. **Roll-the-ball** — roll a soft ball to baby, encourage push back. Materials: soft ball. Builds: gross motor, turn-taking seed. Age 8-12m.

## Band 1-2 years
9. **Stacking cups / steel bowls** — nest and stack household bowls, knock down, restack. Builds: hand-eye, size ordering. Age 12-24m.
10. **Sorting by colour** — sort blocks/bottle caps into two bowls by colour. Materials: household caps, 2 bowls. Builds: categorization, early maths. Age 18-24m.
11. **Posting box** — drop objects through a slot cut in a box lid. Materials: box, safe objects. Builds: fine motor, cause-effect. Age 12-18m.
12. **Water pouring** — pour water between two small vessels over a tray. Materials: 2 cups, tray. Builds: coordination, practical life. Age 18-24m. Video: yes.
13. **First pretend play** — feed a doll, put teddy to sleep. Materials: doll/teddy. Builds: imagination, empathy seed. Age 18-24m.
14. **Movement to music** — clap, stamp, sway to a song. Materials: none. Builds: gross motor, rhythm. Age 12-24m.
15. **First words picture point** — point to pictures in a board book, name them, wait for baby to try. Materials: board book. Builds: vocabulary. Age 12-24m.

## Band 2-3 years
16. **Practical-life pouring and spooning** — spoon dal/rice between bowls (supervised). Builds: fine motor, independence, focus. Age 2-3y. Video: yes.
17. **Colour hunt** — "find something red in the room." Builds: colour recognition, observation. Age 2-3y.
18. **Shape sorting/matching** — match household lids to their containers. Builds: shapes, problem-solving. Age 2-3y.
19. **Threading** — thread large beads/pasta on a lace. Materials: pasta, string. Builds: fine motor, focus. Age 2.5-3y.
20. **Pretend kitchen/shop** — role-play cooking or a sabzi shop. Builds: language, imagination, social. Age 2-3y.
21. **Mark-making** — free scribbling with fat crayons on paper. Builds: pre-writing grip. Age 2-3y.
22. **Sort and count** — count out 3 spoons, 3 katoris while laying the table. Builds: early numeracy, helping. Age 2.5-3y.
23. **Story act-out** — act out a simple story with actions and sounds. Builds: language, sequencing. Age 2.5-3y.

## Band 3-4 years
24. **Pre-writing lines and patterns** — trace lines, zigzags, circles with a finger then a crayon. Builds: pre-writing control. Age 3-4y. Video: yes.
25. **Counting games** — count steps, count rotis, one-to-one touch counting. Builds: number sense. Age 3-4y.
26. **Letter sounds through play** — "what starts with mmm... mummy, mango." Builds: phonemic awareness. Age 3-4y.
27. **Sorting and patterns** — make a red-blue-red-blue pattern with blocks. Builds: logic, sequencing. Age 3-4y.
28. **Practical-life dressing** — buttons, zips, pouring own water. Builds: independence. Age 3-4y.
29. **Junk-modelling craft** — build from boxes, caps, paper. Builds: creativity, fine motor. Age 3-4y.
30. **Story with questions** — read a story, ask "what happens next / why." Builds: comprehension, thinking. Age 3-4y.

## Band 4-6 years
31. **Name-writing practice** — trace then copy own name. Builds: writing, identity. Age 4-6y.
32. **Simple sums with objects** — add and take away using kaju/beads. Builds: early arithmetic. Age 4-6y.
33. **Rhyming and word games** — "which words sound the same?" Builds: literacy. Age 4-6y.
34. **Sequencing a story** — put story cards in order, retell. Builds: logic, narrative. Age 4-6y.
35. **Responsibility jobs** — a small daily chore (water a plant, lay napkins). Builds: responsibility, habit. Age 4-6y.
36. **Show-and-tell / speaking** — describe a favourite object to the family for a minute. Builds: confidence, speaking (bridges to Skilling). Age 4-6y.
