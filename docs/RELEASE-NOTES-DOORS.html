ParentVeda · release notes for product review

The doors, against the briefs they were built from

Trying to Conceive · Pregnancy · Parenting · Skilling. What each brief asked for, what is built, what is not — and where the same content outside a door was left behind when the door was brought up to its brief. 22 September 2026.
What was checked, and against what.
  • Briefs: the 41 PDFs in Downloads\door-pdf — TTC 9, Pregnancy 8, Parenting 12, Skilling 12 — plus the two home change-lists and the bottom-nav list supplied separately.
  • Surfaces: each stage's door and each stage's V3 home. The Classic / V1 homes are kept for revert and are deliberately out of scope. Search, the Tools hub and the Explore sheet were checked wherever they hold a second copy of something a brief touched.
  • Every point is numbered 1–124. Feedback can be given as a point number.
  • Status words: Built to the brief · Changed deliberately, reason on the record · Partial · Not built · Unwired the door is right, something else is not · Blocked can't be reviewed yet · Decided already settled, please don't re-open.
  • Section 1 is the only part that needs a decision before the rest is worth reading. Sections 2–5 are per-stage compliance; section 6 is the two home lists point by point; section 7 is the content still owed, counted.

1. Read this first — what needs attention, not what already agrees

Twenty points. The first seven are the class of problem worth the most attention: the door matches its brief and the same thing somewhere else in the app does not, so a reviewer sees two versions of one feature depending on where they tapped.
